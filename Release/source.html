Part 1 - The World

The Moon is a backdrop. It is in the Terrace and the Cistern.

The Terrace is a room. The description is "A great terrace in the Palace of Herod, set above the banqueting-hall. The full moon shines brightly.[if Grate is open] A low circular wall of green bronze has a metal grate leaning against it.[otherwise] A low circular wall of green bronze surrounds a metal grate."

The wall of green bronze is a thing in the Terrace. It is fixed in place. It is scenery. "The wall encircles the mouth of a disused cistern which extends under the terrace."

The Grate is a door. It is below the Terrace and above the Cistern. It is scenery. The Grate is closed and locked. The description is "[if closed]A heavy rusting metal grate. It covers the mouth of an old cistern.[otherwise]The grate lies against the wall of green bronze." The Silver Charger unlocks the Grate.

Mouth of the Cistern is scenery in the Terrace. "Without climbing down into the cistern, all you can see of it here is the opening."

The Cistern is a room. The Cistern is below the Grate. The description is "The cistern is dark. Only the moon's faint beams penetrate the gloom. A dark mass lies huddled on the floor."

Princess Salome is a female in the Terrace.

The Silver Charger is a container. The description is "A wide basin of elaborately embossed and chased silver."

The Silver Charger is carried by Princess Salome.

The Head of Iokanaan is a thing. It is nowhere.

Dark mass is a thing in the Cistern. It is scenery. It is fixed in place.

The player carries the heavy sword. The description of the heavy sword is "A long, heavy sword, suitable for decapitation."

The player carries a ring. The description of the ring is "A gold ring set with a large black onyz. This is your warrant from the King to perform an execution."

Part 2 - The Play

When play begins:

say "SALOMÉ (kneeling) I would that they presently bring me in a silver charger....[line break][line break]HEROD (laughing) In a silver charger? Surely yes, in a silver charger. She is charming, is she not? What is it you would have in a silver charger, O sweet and fair Salomé, you who are fairer than all the daughters of Judæa? What would you have them bring thee in a silver charger? Tell me. Whatsoever it may be, they shall give it you. My treasures belong to thee. What is it, Salomé?[line break][line break]SALOMÉ (rising) The head of Iokanaan.";

pause the game.

Instead of examining the Moon:

say "She is like [one of]a woman rising from a tomb[or]a dead woman[or]a little princess who wears a yellow veil, and whose feet are of silver[or]a princess who has little white doves for feet[or]a little piece of money[or]a little silver flower[or]a dead woman who is seeking to cover herself with a shroud[or]a little princess, whose eyes are eyes of amber[or]a mad woman who is seeking everywhere for lovers[or]the moon: that is all[in random order]."

Instead of examining the player:

say "You are Naaman, the King's executioner."

Instead of dropping the heavy sword:

say "It's never a good idea for an executioner to be without thier sword.";

stop the action.

Instead of dropping the ring:

say "Losing the King's death ring would only result in you losing your own head.";

stop the action.

Instead of examining Princess Salome:

if Princess Salome carries the Silver Charger:

say "Flushed--glowing--from her dance and from forcing King Herod to grant her wish. She holds a silver charger out to you, demanding, 'Bring me the head of Iokanaan!'";

else if the player carries the Silver Charger containing the Head of Iokanaan:

say "'Give me the charger!'";

otherwise:

say "'Bring me the head of Iokanaan!' the Princess demands impatiently."

Understand "talk to [someone]" as a mistake ("The Princess's only response is an icy glare.").

Instead of asking Princess Salome for the Silver Charger when the Princess Salome is carrying the Silver Charger:

move the Silver Charger to the player;

say "You bow to the Princess, and take the charger."

Instead of searching the grate:

say "You peer down through the grate, but all you can see is vague shadows."

Instead of opening or pushing the grate:

if the grate is locked and the player carries the Silver Charger:

say "You push the Grate to one side.";

now the grate is unlocked;

now the grate is open;

else if the grate is open:

say "You have already moved the grate from the mouth of the cistern.";

otherwise:

say "No one has ordered you to move the grate over the cistern, where the Prophet Iokanaan is imprisoned.";

stop the action.

Before going down:

if the Grate is locked:

say "No one has ordered you to go down into the cistern, where the Prophet Iokanaan is imprisoned.";

stop the action;

otherwise:

continue the action.

After going down in the Cistern:

if the Silver Charger contains the Head of Iokanaan:

say "The cistern is dark. Only the moon's faint beams penetrate the gloom. A dark mass lies huddled on the floor.";

otherwise:

if the player carries the Silver Charger:

say "After climbing down into the cistern, your work is quickly accomplished. Iokanaan offers no resistance as you push him face down, and, with a single, heavy blow, cleave off his head.";

now the Head of Iokanaan is in the Silver Charger.

Instead of examining or taking the Dark mass:

say "You'll dispose of that later."

After going up to the Terrace:

say "You clamber out of the cistern."

Instead of going nowhere from the Terrace:

say "You really should not leave the vicinity of the terrace."

Instead of giving the Head of Iokanaan to Princess Salome:

say "The Princess glares at you. 'I want the head of Iokanaan brought to me IN the charger!'";

stop the action.

Instead of giving or showing the Silver Charger to Princess Salome:

if the Silver Charger contains the Head of Iokanaan:

move the Silver Charger to Princess Salome;

end the story finally saying "The Princess eagerly takes the silver charger with its bloody prize. She leans her head towards it slowly. [line break][line break]Her eyes blazing, her face comes closer and closer to the lifeless face.... [line break][line break]You look away and up: the moon is like a great silver charger, horribly stained.";

otherwise:

say "The Princess will not be pleased if you give her a headless charger.";

stop the action.
